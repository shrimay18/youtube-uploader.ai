AI YouTube Manager

A studio that works while you don’t.

Drop a video and get a ready-to-publish, SEO-optimized YouTube post — title, description, tags, chapters and thumbnail — then publish or schedule it to as many of your channels as you like. It runs on your own device, with your own keys.

🎬

Video → publish-ready post

Transcribes your video and writes a title, description, tags, chapters and thumbnail tuned to what actually ranks.

📡

Publish to many channels

Connect as many YouTube channels as you own and send the same video to all of them — review each or upload directly.

🔐

Private by design

Your API keys and YouTube tokens are encrypted on your device and never sent to us. We can’t see them.

🗓️

Schedule & stagger

Pick a publish time, or batch a whole Drive folder and space the uploads out automatically.

🎯

Real competitor research

Titles and tags are built from the top-ranking videos for your topic — not generic AI filler.

🧩

Your own AI keys

Bring your own Gemini, Groq or Anthropic keys. You’re in control of the models and the costs.

🔒

Your data stays yours.

youtube_manager.ai runs locally on your device. It uploads to YouTube on your behalf using a connection you authorize, and it never transfers your videos, transcripts, API keys or YouTube tokens to our servers. We only keep the basics needed to run your account. Read the full privacy policy →
